Ultra SMS Gateway

Send SMS through your own phones, at the price of your bundles.

A self-hosted gateway: your applications queue messages, Android phones send them with their SIM cards.

  • Your SIM, your price. Every SMS uses your operator's bundles instead of a paid SMS API.
  • Nothing gets lost. Messages wait in the queue until a phone is online, and expire when they are no longer useful.
  • Everything is audited. Each status change is recorded; message texts are erased once sent.
